# Translated and Shared Blogs

This section presents three AWS articles that I studied, translated, summarized, and shared during my internship. The topics cover scheduled automation, AI/ML application integration, and Disaster Recovery strategy on AWS.

## Selected articles

### [Blog 1 - Amazon EventBridge Scheduler: A Small but Useful Service for AWS Projects](3.1-Blog1/)
The article explains how **Amazon EventBridge Scheduler** can invoke Lambda and other AWS services on schedule without maintaining EC2 or a custom background service. It also covers scheduling patterns, retries, dead-letter queues, and a lab that invoked Lambda every 30 minutes.

### [Blog 2 - Integrating AI into Applications with Amazon SageMaker and AWS AI Services](3.2-Blog2/)
The article introduces the two main groups of AWS AI/ML tools, the process of preparing data, training, and deploying a model with Amazon SageMaker, and rapid AI integration through APIs and SDKs.

### [Blog 3 - Building a Disaster Recovery Strategy and Distributed Network on AWS](3.3-Blog3/)
The article explains RTO and RPO, AWS Elastic Disaster Recovery block-level replication, centralized connectivity through AWS Transit Gateway, and the steps required to test a DR scenario.
